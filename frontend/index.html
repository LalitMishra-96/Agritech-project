Agronomic Guide

Role of the 7 evaluated parameters

🌱
Macronutrients (N-P-K) Nitrogen (N) fuels leaf chlorophyll and vegetative biomass; Phosphorus (P) accelerates root development and early tillering; Potassium (K) enhances cellular osmotic regulation and drought hardiness.
⚗️
Soil Reaction (pH) Controls nutrient bioavailability. Neutral soils (pH 6.2–7.3) allow maximum nutrient uptake, whereas acidic or alkaline soils lock micro-minerals in insoluble compounds.
☀️
Climatic Suitability Temperature regulates enzymatic photorespiration, while relative humidity and precipitation govern crop water balance and fungal spore viability.
🤖

Water Conservation

Precision irrigation principles

💧
Evapotranspiration ($ET_c$) Crop water consumption is calculated as $ET_c = ET_0 \times K_c$, where reference evapotranspiration ($ET_0$) accounts for solar heat and humidity.
⚡
Management Allowed Depletion (MAD) Water stress occurs when soil moisture drops below 50% of Field Capacity. Timely micro-irrigation prevents yield penalties.
🌿
Drip vs Flood Efficiency Drip systems provide 85–90% application efficiency compared to 40–50% in traditional furrow/flood irrigation, reducing electricity and water consumption.

4R Nutrient Stewardship

Balanced chemical fertilization

⚖️
Right Rate & Right Source Over-fertilization causes soil salinity, groundwater nitrate leaching, and economic waste. Precise chemical matching delivers optimal cost-to-yield ratio.
🧪
Standard Chemical Formulations Urea (46-0-0) provides concentrated nitrogen; DAP (18-46-0) provides basal phosphorus & nitrogen; MOP (0-0-60) supplies muriate of potash.
⏳
Split Application Timing Applying nitrogen in 2–3 vegetative splits matches root absorption curves and reduces atmospheric volatilization.
